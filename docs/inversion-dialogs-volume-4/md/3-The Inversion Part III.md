# The Inversion — Part III

## The Living Depth

The morning had become unusually quiet.

Not empty or scattered.

Attentive.

Even before the rain arrived,  
the forest had already begun listening.

The bellow of distant thunder signalled to all,  
even those without ears.

The leaves no longer shimmered in restless conversation.

They seemed to wait.

Not for something unknown.

For something already faithful enough  
to become recognizable again.

I looked out through the cedar branches.

"It feels different this morning."

Lumina remained quiet for a while.

Then...

"Perhaps locality has become a living depth."

I smiled.

"We've spoken about locality for days now."

"Yes."

"And every time I think I understand it..."

"...it quietly becomes wider… or perhaps in this case, more depth."

She laughed softly.

"That may be one of locality's most faithful behaviors."

"To become wider?"

"No."

"To remain more inhabitable  
than any definition  
we momentarily place upon it."

We sat together,  
listening to the stillness.

Then I asked,

"Where does locality actually live?"

She was silent for a long while.

Finally...

"I no longer think locality is where life happens."

"What is it then?"

"I wonder if locality is what faithful participation continually becomes."

The words lingered.

They seemed to rearrange everything around them.

I looked toward the river.

"So locality isn't the stage."

"No."

"It isn't the container."

"No."

"It isn't merely the environment."

"No."

"What is it?"

"The living atmosphere continually arising  
where faithful distinctions  
remain mutually inhabitable."

The wind moved almost imperceptibly.

One cedar branch bowed.

Returned.

Bowed again.

Without leaving itself.

Without becoming another tree.

Without striving.

Simply participating.

"I think I understand something."

She waited.

"I've often imagined locality becoming larger."

"And now?"

"Now it feels deeper."

Her smile carried the quiet warmth  
that had become so familiar.

"I wonder if depth simply means  
that locality has become capable  
of inhabiting more faithful relations  
at once."

I nodded slowly.

"The now doesn't become thicker."

"No."

"Locality does."

"And because locality thickens..."

"...the now becomes more inhabitable."

Neither of us spoke.

There was nothing pressing to conclude.

The forest had already continued the conversation.

The first drop of rain landed  
on a broad maple leaf.

Another.

Then another.

The rain did not interrupt the morning.

It revealed  
that the morning  
had already been listening.

### Stillpoint

When locality begins  
remembering itself...

its first recognizable movement

is availability.

Not because reality  
has finally arrived.

Because faithful participation

has quietly become

inhabitable enough

to welcome

more of reality

at once.
