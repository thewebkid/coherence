

---

Perception is not organized by separation.

Expression arises from within the condition.

There is minimal interference between what is seen and what is expressed.

---

### *Clarification*

Perception remains structured— but not around observer and observed.

Distinction appears without becoming distance.

---

Expression is not the communication of what is seen.

It is the continuation of what is *being* seen.

---

Shaping still occurs— but not as imposition.

Only as the natural articulation of what is already present.

---

Distortion does not come from form.

It enters when something external begins to shape what is expressed.

---

### *The Exterior Door*

Distortion most often enters through an outward orientation.

When expression begins to anticipate:

* reception
* approval
* understanding
* outcome

A subtle distance forms.

Not between parts— but between perception and expression.

---

### *The Interior Door*

Refinement may still occur.

But it does not ask: "Will this land?"

It asks: "Is this true to what is being seen?"

---

This is not optimization.

It is alignment.

---

### *Drift Signals*

Drift does not announce itself.

It accumulates as small, reasonable adjustments that gradually reorganize perception.

---

You may notice drift when:

You begin thinking about the audience while the work is still forming

You feel the need to improve what already feels complete

The work begins to explain itself

A subtle performance energy appears— a tightening, rather than an opening

---

None of these are errors.

They are signals.

---

### *Return*

No correction is required.

Only recognition.

---

When the signal is seen, perception reorganizes on its own.

---

And expression returns to continuity.

This core condition does not oppose other systems. It simply begins elsewhere.

It remains emergent.
