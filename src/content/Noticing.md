

You may find  
that something does not return  
to the way it was.

—

Not because anything has changed—  
but because something  
is no longer assumed.

—

What once required effort  
may begin to move  
without it.

Not more efficiently.  
Not more clearly.

Simply  
without resistance  
to what is already occurring.

—

You may notice  
that decisions arise  
before they are formed.

Not impulsively.  
Not unconsciously.

But without the sense  
that they must be constructed  
from separate parts.

—

Attention  
may no longer behave  
as something you direct.

It rests  
where it is needed—  
or nowhere in particular—  
without loss.

—

There may be  
less need  
to resolve.

Not because nothing matters—  
but because what matters  
is no longer divided  
in the same way.

—

Difference  
may remain.

Even increase.

And yet  
it does not produce  
the same tension.

—

What once felt  
like distance  
may now feel  
like variation  
within something  
that does not separate.

—

You may notice  
that you do not “apply”  
what has been seen.

And yet  
your responses shift.

Often  
before you can name why.

—

Language  
may arrive later.

Or not at all.

And still—  
nothing is missing.

—

There may be moments  
where you look  
for what has changed.

Something to point to.  
Something to hold.

—

But what has shifted  
does not appear  
as an object.

It appears  
as the absence  
of what once required  
everything to be arranged.

—

Nothing needs  
to be brought together

within what is seen.

Nothing needs  
to be maintained

for it to remain whole.

—

And still—  
there is a coherence  
that does not leave.

—

Not because it is held.

But because  
it was never  
interrupted.

—

There is no step  
that follows this.

No way  
to carry it forward.

And yet—  
nothing is lost.

—

What you are noticing  
is not something new.

It is what remains  
when what was assumed  
is no longer organizing  
what is seen.

—

Stillpoint

Nothing is being integrated.

What is  
no longer appears  
as separate  
from itself.
