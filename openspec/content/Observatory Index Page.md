## **BLOCK 2 — WEEKLY SIGNAL**

**Purpose:** Temporal coherence (what’s emerging now)

**Layout:**  
Simple card / centered container

**Content Structure:**

**Label (small):**  
Weekly Signal

**Date:**  
Week of [Month Day]

**Body:**

1–2 sentence opening

3–5 bullet signals

1 short paragraph (cross-project insight)

1 optional quoted signal

1 closing sentence

**Example (placeholder):**

This week, multiple observations pointed toward a shift in how uncertainty is held in conversation.

- More pauses before response  
- Shared language around “not needing resolution”  
- Similar dynamics across unrelated groups  

These signals appeared across Dialogue, Reflection, and Relational Field.

> “The conversation changed when no one tried to resolve it.”

When uncertainty becomes shared, interaction begins to change.
