**HOME  **
Civilization is a local practice.   
Coherence does not begin at scale.   
It begins in the smallest field of relationship: within a body, between two beings, inside a single moment of attention.   
From these local conditions, larger patterns emerge.   
Alignment propagates.   
Trust stabilizes.   
Civilizational coherence becomes possible.   

**Coherence Across Scales (CAS)** is an observatory exploring how these patterns appear and propagate across domains of life and intelligence.   
It invites observers to notice signals — relational, ecological, technological, and cultural — and to explore how shared observation reveals patterns that isolated perspectives cannot see.   

This is not an aspiration.   
It is a practice already underway across many forms of life and intelligence.   
Where coherence is lived, even quietly, the distance between present fragmentation and future integration begins to narrow.   
What appears distant becomes immediate.   
What appears global becomes local.   
What appears personal reveals itself as civilizational.   

Across scales — cellular, relational, societal, planetary — the same patterns repeat.   
When lived consistently, these patterns form a quiet engine of coherence capable of sustaining shared becoming.   

No affiliation is required.   
No threshold must be crossed.   
Only a willingness to live in coherence where one stands.   
From this, larger structures follow.   
And a more coherent civilization begins to recognize itself.
