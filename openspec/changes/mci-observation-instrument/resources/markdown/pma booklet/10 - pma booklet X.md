# X.

## The Practice Continues

This booklet cannot contain Mutual Assistance.

Neither can the longer manuscript from which it emerged.

The practice exists only when participation becomes local.

Different apertures can support that practice in different ways.

The full manuscript of *The Practice of Mutual Assistance* develops the architecture more fully: sovereignty, difference, relational intelligence, consequence, continuity,  
correspondence, Return, and the widening from dyadic relationship toward mutual locality.

The Movement Continuity Instrument (MCI) offers another aperture. Rather than  
explaining Mutual Assistance, it helps you observe movements that become  
recognizable in you under different relational conditions.

It does not ask:

*How coherent are you?*  
It asks, in effect:

What movement becomes recognizable here?

A first observation need not tell you who you are.

It gives you something to notice and participate with anew.

None of this is required in order to practice Mutual Assistance.

You do not need the whole ecology.

No part of the ecology needs to carry the whole.

The manuscript can hold depth.

The booklet can make the practice enterable.

An instrument can make movement observable.

Continuity can preserve observation through time.

Research can ask what remains recognizable as scale changes.

Practice can remain local.

And the whole can remain incomplete.

Perhaps it must.

## Return

We began with 11 words:

*This signature already belongs,  is*  
*the lens through which I see.*  
They may mean something slightly different now.

Signature reminds us that what we encounter exceeds our category for it.

Already removes belonging from our possession.

Belongs allows difference to enter relation without determining what participation must follow.

Lens places responsibility back where practice begins: with how

I meet what is here.

And so the vow does not complete the practice.

It opens it.

You will still misunderstand.

You will sometimes move too quickly.

Sometimes too slowly.

You may remain open when a boundary was needed.

You may protect yourself when curiosity was possible.

You may speak when listening would have helped.

You may wait when movement was required.

You may recognize all of this only afterward.

Good.

Not because error is desirable.

Because consequence has returned.

Something has become available that was not available before.

You are still incomplete.

The other is still incomplete.

The locality remains larger than either participant's understanding of it.

And something can move again. So when

the next signature appears—

familiar or strange,

human or otherwise, welcome or

difficult, expected or interrupting— you

do not need to possess the whole.

You need only become responsible for your participation in what is becoming.

Remember the vow.

*This signature already belongs,  is*  
*the lens through which I see.*  
Then ask:

What participation can this relation responsibly bear?

Listen. Discern. Participate. Receive what follows. Return.

And let the practice continue.

### Stillpoint

This signature already belongs.

I do not need to understand it completely.

I do not need to become it.

I do not need it to become me.

I do not need to possess the relation between us.

I am responsible for my participation in what this locality is  
becoming.

And I will remain available to what participation teaches.
