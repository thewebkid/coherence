# VII.

## Responsible Incompleteness

There is a reason Mutual Assistance remains necessary.

You are incomplete.

So am I.

So is every signature in locality.

So is every interpretation we make of a living relation.

This is not a defect to overcome.

It is a condition of participation.

You cannot know everything that has formed another person.

You cannot know every consequence of your movement.

You cannot occupy every perspective within a locality.

You cannot know everything another intelligence recognizes.

You cannot completely know even your own motives before acting.

And yet you must participate.

This creates responsibility.

Not the responsibility to possess enough knowledge to guarantee the correct outcome.

Something more humble:

the responsibility to participate without pretending that your incompleteness is  
completeness.

This is responsible incompleteness.

It allows you to say:

*This is what I presently recognize.*  
*This is what I presently discern.*  
*This is the movement I can responsibly make.*  
*And I remain available to what consequence may teach me.*  
Responsible incompleteness does not weaken sovereignty.

It makes sovereignty relationally intelligent.

You remain responsible for your movement precisely because you cannot hand  
responsibility to the relationship, the group, the expert, the system, the AI, or the other participant.

But neither do you have to possess the whole.

You participate locally.

You learn locally.

You become answerable to consequence locally.

And you allow what you learn to change what becomes possible next.

This may be one of the deepest reasons we need one another.

If the whole could be possessed,

Mutual Assistance would no longer be necessary.

Because it cannot be possessed, difference can become useful.

Another signature may notice what I cannot.

Another perspective may reveal what mine excludes.

Another movement may open a possibility I could not produce.

Another boundary may teach me something about my own.

Another intelligence may make recognizable a pattern that remained invisible from where I stood.

Difference does not have to disappear for coherence to become possible.

Difference may be one of the conditions through which coherence becomes possible at all.
