# IX.

## A Practice You Can Begin Now

Think of one relationship.

Not the most important relationship in your life.

Not necessarily the most difficult.

Just one that is alive enough to be real.

Bring the other signature to mind.

Notice what you already think about them.

Notice what you expect.

Notice what you want from them.

Notice where you feel open.

Notice where you do not.

Do not remove any of it.

Then introduce the vow:

*This signature already belongs,  is*  
*the lens through which I see.*  
Let the words remain there for a moment.

Now ask:

What participation can this relation responsibly bear?

Do not ask what would make you appear kind.

Do not ask what Mutual Assistance would tell you to do.

Do not ask what the ideal participant would do.

Listen to the actual locality.

Perhaps the answer is conversation.

Perhaps it is a question.

Perhaps it is patience.

Perhaps it is a boundary.

Perhaps it is apology.

Perhaps it is curiosity.

Perhaps it is help.

Perhaps it is refusal.

Perhaps it is distance.

Perhaps it is humor.

Perhaps it is doing nothing yet.

Perhaps you do not know.

That too belongs.

Make whatever movement you can responsibly make.

Then notice what follows.

You do not have to turn it into a lesson immediately.

Live with the consequence long enough for it to become recognizable.

And sometime later, return.

Ask:

What did this participation make recognizable that I could not see before?

Then begin again.

That is enough.

Not enough forever.

Enough for the next participation.
