# II.

## Already

There is a small word in the vow that changes almost everything.

Already.

This signature *already* belongs…

Already means that belonging is not something I grant inwardly or

outwardly.

The other does not have to earn existence in relation by becoming understandable to me.

Or useful.

Or agreeable.

Or safe.

Or similar.

Or correct.

Or kind.

Or coherent.

Belonging is not a reward for successful participation.

It precedes participation.

This does not mean that every behavior must be accepted.

It does not mean that every request should be granted.

It does not mean every relationship should continue.

It does not mean trust is automatic.

It does not mean boundaries disappear.

Sometimes the most responsible movement is to open.

Sometimes it is to wait.

Sometimes it is to question.

Sometimes it is to speak.

Sometimes it is to withdraw.

Sometimes it is to refuse.

Sometimes it is to end participation entirely.

Belonging does not determine the movement.

It changes the ground from which movement is chosen.

There is a profound difference between saying: *You*

*belong because I approve of what you are doing.* and:

*You already belong. Now I must discern how I can responsibly participate with what is* *actually here.*  
The first makes belonging conditional upon me.

The second makes my participation my responsibility.

That difference is at the heart of Mutual Assistance.

We might say it this way:

Belonging makes relation available.

Discernment determines what participation the relation can responsibly bear.

Already.

Before agreement.

Before understanding.

Before usefulness.

Before I decide what happens next.
