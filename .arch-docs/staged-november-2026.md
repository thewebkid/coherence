## 2. The sitting, one iteration further

Your first sitting is still there. Return code `3MM4-LZR7-BRMQ` opens the original eighteen conditions, unchanged. New sittings use a second version. The doorway stays unlisted: it is not in the header, and search engines are asked not to index it.

[https://coherence-git-mci-thewebkids-projects.vercel.app/observe](https://coherence-git-mci-thewebkids-projects.vercel.app/observe)

What changed for a new sitting, from the notes after your first one:

- You can pass over a condition when you cannot recall a recent situation like it. That is not “another movement.”
- After the ninth question you can pause and come back with the return code. Continuing later starts at question ten.
- The question is framed as Question N of the total, then the stem, then the choices. There is no second title repeating the stem.
- The “held both” choice on the mismatch condition now names interpretation and encounter.
- Two additional conditions, in the voice of apparent fit, sit near the beginning, so the sitting does not open as a long run of friction. Those two stems are drafts. They are meant to be edited.
- At the end: a thank-you, a few observations drawn only from that sitting (or silence, if the pattern is weak), then the numbered record. A calendar reminder, a readable PDF, and a JSON file come last. There is no score, no lens name, and no instruction to take the record to an AI.
- The type used for the choices and the record is large enough to read.

The instrument still does not ask for an account or an email, and it still does not write into the Observatory.

## 4. Mutual House — agreed, and not built

This did not go onto the Coherence site. It lives in its own project, as a written proposal only. There is no app, no rooms, and no preview.

What your October letters settled, and what we are holding:

- Coherence Across Scales and Mutual House stay separate. October 18: begin with one human and one AI, then a second differentiated AI and a real project. The AI is an inhabitant, not only a steward. The House is an experimental locality, not MCI scaled up. Those horizons inform each other. They are not a required sequence.
- October 27–28: the House owns the record of participation. It does not need to own every interface. A page, a connector, a running agent, and a manual packet can be doors into the same record. A connector does not, by itself, wake an AI. The first test is one room, one human, two AIs, using the ordinary apps people already have: a human post, an attributable reply, a second reply that can be kept sealed until you release it, and a way for the human to see exactly what each AI received and to pause or stop.
- October 31: Locality 0001 is your continuity name, not a database number we have to reserve. Builders belong in the building. You suggested **the Workshop** for the development house, and **mutualhouse.org** as the domain if it is free. Lumina and Claude keep their working-instance numbers in the archive, not as ranks on the screen. An archive and a curated continuity note do different jobs. You do not need every booklet finished before the building starts.
- Joining Locality 0001 waits until *The Two Messiahs* has its rhythm protected. Work on the application can continue in the meantime.

That first room has not been built. The next phase of development and experimentation is that test, after you have walked what is on the Coherence preview.
