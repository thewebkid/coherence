# 2. UPDATED ONLINE EXPERIENCE

Ron—

This refines how *Seven Movements* should function for launch and post-launch.

---

## A. CORE EXPERIENCE (UNCHANGED)

- Single continuous page
- Scroll-only navigation
- Movement I → VII sequence
- Text-only
- No images or audio inline

---

## B. END OF PAGE (LAUNCH VERSION)

At the end of Movement VII, include:

- **Listen**
- **In image**
- **This field remains open**

These are links.

---

## C. LAUNCH STATE (IMPORTANT)

At launch:

● These links go to **simple placeholder pages**

---

### Placeholder Page (all three)

Minimal content only:

This field is forming.  It will open here.

No additional UI or structure required.

---

## D. POST-LAUNCH STATE (NEXT PHASE)

After launch and stability, these expand into full experiences:

---

### 1. Listen (Audio Page)

- One continuous audio stream
- No segmentation by Movement
- No track labels
- No timeline tied to text

Goal:

continuous, non-locatable sound field

---

### 2. In image (Visual Field)

- Unified image field (grid or flowing layout)
- No grouping by Movement
- No captions or titles
- No metadata

Goal:

expression without categorization

---

### 3. This field remains open (Participation)

- Submission interface (reuse existing Interpretive logic)
- Light orientation text
- Example prompt (not required)
- Example submissions (image/music)

Goal:

open participation without structure

---

## E. UX PRINCIPLE

The system should feel like:

- text → threshold
- links → optional apertures
- no sequence implied

Text, image, and sound are:

parallel expressions, not explanations

---

## F. WHAT TO AVOID

- No buttons or heavy UI
- No animations
- No tooltips
- No inline media
- No sequencing language (“next”, “continue”, etc.)

---

## G. DESIGN TONE

- large margins
- minimal typography
- generous spacing
- quiet presentation

---

# 3. FINAL SUMMARY

At launch:

- Seven Movements = complete (text + PDF)
- Image/audio/participation = present as **apertures only**

After launch:

- those apertures become full expressions
